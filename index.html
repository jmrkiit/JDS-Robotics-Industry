About JDS Robotics Industry


JDS Robotics Industry is a registered manufacturing company dedicated to advancing education through Artificial Intelligence, Robotics, Coding, STEM Education, IoT, and Automation technologies. The company develops innovative educational products and provides complete Robotics, AI, and STEM laboratory solutions for schools and educational institutions. With a strong emphasis on practical learning and hands-on experience, JDS Robotics Industry helps students build future-ready technical skills and explore emerging technologies. JDS Robotics Industry offers comprehensive support including laboratory setup, curriculum guidance, teacher training, project-based learning resources, and access to more than 100 Robotics, AI, and STEM projects. The company is proud to manufacture ZED Certified products and maintains a business collaboration with ACEBOTT Ltd., China. Through innovation, quality, and industry-oriented education, JDS Robotics Industry is committed to inspiring the next generation of inventors, engineers, innovators, and technology leaders.

About the Founder

Jagmehender Sheoran is the Founder of JDS Robotics Industry, a registered company specializing in Artificial Intelligence and Robotics solutions. With over 15 years of experience spanning education and industry, he combines academic knowledge with practical expertise. He has authored two international e-books and published two internationally recognized research papers, reflecting his commitment to innovation and advancement in AI and Robotics.
